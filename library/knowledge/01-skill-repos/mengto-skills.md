# MengTo/Skills

Roughly 140 skills across five groups. None installed. By far the largest and most
unusual repo in the set.

## Structure

| Group | Count | Character |
|---|---:|---|
| `web-design` | ~90 | **Named visual styles and effects**, not processes |
| `game-development` | ~20 | Three.js / web game systems — out of scope |
| `codex` | ~19 | Meta-skills: turning articles, videos and web techniques *into* skills |
| `media` | 2 | Unsplash and Aura asset sourcing |
| `ui` | 1 | `design-first-ui-prompting` |

## What makes `web-design` different from everything installed

These are not "improve this" processes. They are **named looks** — each one a specific,
reproducible aesthetic:

`clean-minimal-beige-light-mode` · `dark-glass-clean-layout` ·
`documentary-brutalist-agency` · `editorial-portfolio-chapters` ·
`funky-purple-container-tech` · `high-contrast-skeuomorphic-clean` ·
`light-mode-paper-technical` · `mesh-gradient-dark-blue-clean` ·
`orange-clean-paper-saas` · `solar-duotone-bold` · `tech-green-dark-mode-modern` ·
`blue-laser-clean-glass-layout` · `nested-container-clean-agency` ·
`technical-wireframe-info-layout` · `image-first-grid-layout` ·
`agency-grid-layout-minimal` · `book-serif-index` · `editorial-tech` ·
`bright-green-tech-system-webgl` · `dark-blue-contrasting-clean`

Effect-level skills: `progressive-blur`, `liquid-metal-border`, `dither-background`,
`css-alpha-masking`, `css-border-gradient`, `beam-glow-states`, `corner-lasers`,
`corner-diagonals`, `masked-reveal`, `staggered-word-reveal`,

`container-lines`, `thinking-orbs`, `falling-leaves`, `mesh-gradient-dark-blue-clean`.

Stack-level: `gsap`, `gsap-scrolltrigger-storytelling`,

`build-awwwards-quality-sites`.

**This is the most useful repo for the three-design-systems goal.** Distinct named looks
are precisely what makes three setups produce genuinely different output instead of
three shades of the same default.

## The `codex` group is the sleeper

`article-prompts-to-skills` · `web-technique-to-skill` · `video-to-superprompt` ·
`html-to-interaction-prompts` · `daily-ui-inspiration-capture` ·
`stitched-full-page-capture` · `audit-reference-originality` · `iterate-until-verified` ·
`generate-reference-inspired-brand-worlds` · `optimize-web-animations`

These are **skills that manufacture skills from source material** — the same job
MNEMOSYNE does. Worth reading as prior art on the pipeline itself, not only as tools.
`audit-reference-originality` addresses the exact failure mode of reference-driven
design: producing a copy rather than an influence.
