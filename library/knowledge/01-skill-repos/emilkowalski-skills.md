## What it is for

The highest-signal source in the set for *interface craft*: motion decisions, what to
animate and what not to, easing and duration, interruption, and the invisible details
that separate polished software from working software. `emil-design-eng` carries the
philosophy; `animate` writes the implementation; `review-animations` and
`improve-animations` critique existing motion; `animation-vocabulary` is a reverse
glossary that turns "the bouncy thing when a popover opens" into "pop in".

## The division of labour worth remembering

- Building new motion — `animate`
- Critiquing motion that already exists — `review-animations`
- Sweeping a whole codebase — `improve-animations`
- Finding where motion is missing — `find-animation-opportunities`
- Naming an effect you can only describe — `animation-vocabulary`

Using `animate` to critique, or `review-animations` to build, is the common misuse.
