# Inspection checklist

- Capture full-page and key-state evidence when access permits.
- Note viewport and whether motion was observed or inferred.
- Identify the first focal point, reading path, primary action, and credibility signal.
- Describe ratios and relationships before naming styles.
- Treat a reel or compressed video as incomplete evidence; do not assume responsive or accessible behavior.
- Flag any principle whose success depends on bespoke photography, 3D, sound, or a high-end device.
- Prefer reusable rules over adjectives such as premium, bold, cinematic, or clean.
