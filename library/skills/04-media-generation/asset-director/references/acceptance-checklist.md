# Asset acceptance checklist

- Supports a specific content or interaction purpose.
- Matches the approved direction across the full set, not just individually.
- Has sufficient source resolution and intentional crop behavior.
- Avoids embedded text unless localization and accessibility are handled.
- Includes mobile and loading fallback where needed.
- Has provenance, rights, and attribution recorded.
- Has meaningful alt text or is explicitly decorative.
- Does not imitate a living artist or copy a reference’s distinctive expression.
- Has a defined rejection threshold so generation does not loop indefinitely.
