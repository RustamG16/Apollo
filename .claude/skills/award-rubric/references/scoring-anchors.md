# Scoring anchors

- 90–100: Exceptional, coherent, specific, usable, and realistically deliverable with minor refinement.
- 75–89: Strong direction with identifiable weaknesses or production risks.
- 60–74: Promising but generic, inconsistent, underspecified, or risky.
- 40–59: Major strategic, usability, or coherence problems.
- Below 40: Does not answer the approved problem.

Avoid false precision: explain the evidence behind every category score. A fatal delivery risk can outweigh a high creative score.
