# Concept distinction test

Before presenting directions, hide their names, colors, and fonts. If their wireframes, content order, and interaction descriptions still feel interchangeable, they are not distinct enough.

Each concept should be explainable without aesthetic adjectives. Prefer structural statements such as “case-study evidence leads before positioning” or “a single cinematic object anchors progressive disclosure.”

Reject concepts that rely on unavailable media without a credible fallback.
