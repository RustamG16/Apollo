# Evidence rubric

Prefer evidence in this order:

1. Reproducible behavior on the actual page.
2. Source/design-system facts and supplied product requirements.
3. First-party analytics or user research with known scope.
4. Supplied reference analysis.
5. Established usability/accessibility principles.
6. Inference or taste, clearly labeled.

For every high or critical finding, answer: where is it visible, who is affected, what goal is harmed, and what evidence could disprove the interpretation?
