# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Durable product truth. Written once, before the design system, and changed only when the
product changes — not when the design does. `impeccable`'s `init` command owns this file;
write it by hand only when that skill's scripts are unavailable in the host.

## Platform

web / ios / android / adaptive

## Stack

The runtime and the libraries, and one clause on why each is fixed.

## Users

Who decides, what they are deciding, and where they are when they decide it.

## Product Purpose

What the surface is for, and what success looks like as an observable outcome.

## Positioning

How the thing wants to be understood, in one or two sentences.

## Operating Context

Devices, viewports, connection, and any constraint the client has fixed. If the client named
a single target resolution, it goes here and it binds the whole run.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The routes and the primary action.
- What ships as a prototype and must say so on screen.
- Facts that are not supplied and must not be invented.
- Accessibility and motion guarantees that are required rather than optional.

## Brand Commitments

Names, palettes, assets and directions that are binding. State plainly whether the subject is
real; if it is fictional, say where the interface discloses that.
