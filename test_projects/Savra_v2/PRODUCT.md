# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript, explicitly fixed by the approved build plan. GSAP and ScrollTrigger provide progressive motion enhancement.

## Users

Design-aware diners and Vienna visitors seeking an intimate destination restaurant and deciding whether to reserve.

## Product Purpose

Introduce SAVRA through its room, craft, and seasonal dishes, then make reservation intent the clearest next action. Success is a visitor reaching the reservation path with enough confidence and desire to request a table.

## Positioning

SAVRA is presented as a threshold into a nocturnal dining ritual shaped by fire, stone, season, and precise craft.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive on mobile while planning an evening in Vienna or browse on desktop for a more cinematic introduction. The experience must remain understandable without scroll animation and must never trap native scrolling.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One reservation-led landing page.
- Primary action: Reserve a table. Secondary action: Explore the menu ritual.
- Reservation ships as a clearly labelled prototype request form until a booking provider is supplied.
- Address, service days, direct contact, pricing, and reservation lead time are not supplied and must not be invented.
- Reduced-motion, touch, keyboard, and mobile behaviors are required.

## Brand Commitments

The name SAVRA, the supplied restaurant and food imagery, and the approved “Enter the Ritual” direction are binding. The experience is restrained, tactile, architectural, nocturnal, and warm at its center.

## Evidence on Hand

The approved plan is at `.olympus/06-build-plan.md`. Original image and video assets are in `media/`. No testimonials, press, menu pricing, booking provider, verified address, service schedule, or social accounts were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Reservation is always reachable without overpowering the visual narrative.
- Atmosphere comes from real supplied media and editorial pacing, not fabricated claims.
- Motion communicates crossing, focus, or sequence and disappears when it would impede access.
- Unknown restaurant details remain plainly marked as pending confirmation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, native touch scrolling, an accessible reservation dialog, and a complete reduced-motion path are required.
