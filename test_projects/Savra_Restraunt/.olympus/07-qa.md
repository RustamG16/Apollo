## Runtime and accessibility evidence

- Console errors: 0. Console warnings: 0.
- All observed in-view images had `complete=true` and non-zero `naturalWidth`. The below-fold reservation image was initially deferred as intended (`loading="lazy"`) and loaded successfully after navigating to Reserve.
- Full network request logging was not exposed by this browser surface, so a numeric failed-request claim is not made. No broken media or console/network-error symptom was observed.
- Document structure: one header, nav, main, and footer; one H1 followed by section H2s and plate H3s; no duplicate IDs; no unnamed visible controls.
- At 390px, all visible interactive targets measured at least 44×44 CSS pixels after the final fix.
- Color calculations: Vanilla/Slate 6.52:1; Vanilla/Auburn 6.63:1. Primary controls use Vanilla-on-Auburn with a Hunyadi accent border. Hunyadi focus indicators against Slate are 3.21:1, above the 3:1 non-text indicator threshold.
- Overflow confirmation after repair: for all five viewports, `scrollWidth === clientWidth`; at 360px both are 350 CSS pixels after the browser scrollbar.
- Reduced-motion QA mirror: video `display:none`, video paused, zero pin spacers, no hero transform, hero opacity 1. Production CSS/JS also directly queries `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Motion performance review

- `gsap-performance` activated after a working implementation existed.
- Six ScrollTriggers: threshold scrub, three plate entrances, craft entrance, and one room pin. Seven animated narrative areas including the hero timeline.
- Motion uses transform/opacity plus one bounded clip-path; no animated width/height/top/left/margin/padding; no per-frame DOM reads; grouped entrance uses stagger.
- One desktop pin, no tablet/mobile pinning, `scrub: 0.8`, scoped `useGSAP`, `gsap.matchMedia`, cleanup/revert, and a single load refresh.
- No smooth-scroll engine, cursor follower, continuous canvas, WebGL, or persistent global `will-change`.
