## Evidence and fatal risks

### Concept 1 — The Threshold Ritual

- **Strongest evidence-backed advantage:** it uses every strong media family—arrival, plates, room, materials, anonymous process, and table—in the same linear sequence as the required content scope, while retaining a persistent reservation action.
- **Weakest assumption:** the 720p video and 1376px wides can sustain a premium cinematic opening at 1440px without looking soft.
- **Fatal risk, if any:** over-choreography could trap or exhaust mobile/reduced-motion users. The frozen concept avoids this only if the single pin is desktop-only, all content exists in final state without JS, and video has a poster/static path.
- **Rationale:** strongest brand-specific spatial metaphor, clear narrative and CTA, exact responsive plan, and honest disclosure. It loses points for motion/build complexity and dependence on careful crop/contrast execution.
- **Confidence:** medium-high for concept fit; missing evidence is actual browser performance, crops, video appearance, and reservation usability.

### Concept 2 — The Fire Index

- **Strongest evidence-backed advantage:** the portrait/landscape plate pairs and broad dish set directly support a responsive editorial index with excellent scannability.
- **Weakest assumption:** an index-led model will feel like hospitality rather than a design portfolio or menu archive.
- **Fatal risk, if any:** loading too many large plates or treating anchors like filters could harm performance and comprehension. The concept must lazy-load and keep navigation semantically honest.
- **Rationale:** highest browsing utility and strong responsive modularity, but weaker emotional arrival-to-reservation arc and less distinctive use of the threshold/room media.
- **Confidence:** medium-high; missing evidence is image-density performance and whether users prefer scanning over a guided ritual.

### Concept 3 — One Table After Dark

- **Strongest evidence-backed advantage:** the smallest curated media set, central focus order, and repeated table motif give it the clearest conversion path and lowest delivery cost.
- **Weakest assumption:** sparse copy and four full-height acts can communicate a specific fire-led Eastern Mediterranean point of view rather than generic luxury minimalism.
- **Fatal risk, if any:** if the copy or recurring table imagery feels thin, the entire concept loses specificity. Long viewport pacing can also become monotonous on mobile.
- **Rationale:** excellent restraint, accessibility potential, and reservation coherence; it trails Concept 1 because it underuses the distinctive asset breadth and carries greater brand-generic risk.
- **Confidence:** medium; missing evidence is content testing and whether the minimal narrative feels sufficiently rich.

## Recommendation

**Select Concept 1 — The Threshold Ritual.** It has the best evidence fit to the required emotional order and business goal: the supplied doorway and room imagery establish arrival, the plate/process groups earn culinary credibility within the fictional concept, and the destination table turns “Reserve a Table” into a coherent culmination. Implement only its bounded motion contract; preserve static final states and keep the reservation demo explicit.
